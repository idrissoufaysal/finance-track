# Product: Finance Track

## Vision & Purpose
Finance Track is a benchmark-grade personal finance mobile application (React Native / Expo) engineered to deliver effortless clarity over daily, weekly, and monthly cash flow.

## Target Audience
Mobile users seeking a private, zero-bloat, high-craft personal expense and income tracking tool that runs seamlessly on iOS, Android and Web with instantaneous offline-first responsiveness.

## Core Features
1. **Multi-Horizon Granularity**: Instant toggle between Jour (Day), Semaine (Week), and Mois (Month) with contextual bar & trend visualization.
2. **Dual-Theme Fidelity**: High-contrast, Apple HIG-compliant Light Mode ("Forest Wealth") and immersive Dark Mode ("Forest Obsidian"), driven by a single theme source of truth.
3. **Real-Time Financial Metrics**: Dynamic savings rate percentage, daily spending average, cash-flow ratio bar, and net balance.
4. **Frictionless Entry**: Large tabular amount entry sheet, native month-calendar date picker, and optional monthly recurrence.
5. **Fast History Management**: Full transaction history with search, period filters, and swipe-to-delete.
6. **Guided First Run**: A single-CTA onboarding screen shown once on first launch.
7. **Zero-Baseline Privacy**: Starts at a clean 0 FCFA balance with local offline storage, in-memory fallbacks, and a one-tap reset to either zero-baseline or demo data.
