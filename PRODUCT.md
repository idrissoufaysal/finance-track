# Product: Finance Track

## Vision & Purpose
Finance Track is a benchmark-grade personal finance mobile application (React Native / Expo) engineered to deliver effortless clarity over daily, weekly, and monthly cash flow.

## Target Audience
Mobile users seeking a private, zero-bloat, high-craft personal budgeting and expense tracking tool that runs seamlessly on iOS and Android with instantaneous offline-first responsiveness.

## Core Features
1. **Multi-Horizon Granularity**: Instant toggle between Jour (Day), Semaine (Week), and Mois (Month) with contextual bar & trend visualization.
2. **Dual-Theme Fidelity**: High-contrast, Apple HIG-compliant Light Mode ("Forest Wealth") and immersive Dark Mode ("Forest Obsidian").
3. **Real-Time Financial Metrics**: Dynamic savings rate percentage, daily spending average, cash-flow ratio bar, and category ranking leaderboards.
4. **Proactive Budget Guardrails**: Monthly category spending caps with visual amber/coral warning thresholds and remaining balance alerts.
5. **Frictionless Entry**: Large tabular amount entry sheet, category icon chips, payment method tags, and instant calculation.
6. **Zero-Baseline Privacy**: Starts at a clean 0 FCFA balance with local offline storage, in-memory fallbacks, and one-tap reset.
